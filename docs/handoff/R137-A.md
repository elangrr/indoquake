## Deviations and decisions made here
- Statistics `area=` needs the route in apps/web (lane B path). A exports `parseAreaFilters(params)` and
  `getStatistics({..., area})`; wiring the route is a one-liner, asked of lane B in a comment on t_94eddf40. If B did
  not take it, the merge card must, or `?area=` on /api/v1/statistics is ignored.
- packages/notifications stays dependency-free (no package.json change allowed): the worker computes `eventTimeText`
  (formatZoned long) and `areaLabel` with @indoquake/regions and passes them on AlertEvent (extra optional fields beside
  the contract's eventTimeUtc, nearestProvinceSlug, nearestProvinceKm, zone). The 300 km limit is NEAR_SEA_MATCH_KM in
  notifications, pinned to NEAR_SEA_MAX_KM by a worker test.
- backfillRegions returns {scanned, updated, remaining} (spec said {scanned, updated}; remaining added per D24).
  updated counts rows actually written; a row the ingest re-assigned meanwhile is skipped by a version re-check.
- Rows with invalid coordinates get no area but the current version, so the backfill does not loop on them.
- notify.ts derives the area from coordinates through describeLocation (assignArea), as the store does.
- compose already had depends_on migrate service_completed_successfully for web and worker; only REGION_BACKFILL added.
