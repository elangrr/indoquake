## Deviations and decisions made here
- Simplification is own Douglas-Peucker per ring, not mapshaper -simplify: its keep-shapes protects features, not
  rings, so island rings vanished (measured 25.9 km displacement at 150 m). Per ring DP keeps every ring (>= 3 points);
  neighbour borders are simplified separately (gaps/overlaps <= interval: overlap resolves by table order, gaps by snap).
- D21 pairwise overlap is checked as total overlap (sum of the 6 areas minus their union) < 0.1% of the smallest,
  which bounds every pair. City-point check uses the 30 SPEC section 6 points; lookup.test covers all 38 capitals.
- Swapped coordinates (110, -7) return null (latitude out of range), as AC 6 "lat 91 -> null"; SPEC 6 prose said "sea".
- Raw downloads live in packages/regions/data-src/raw/, ignored by packages/regions/.gitignore (root .gitignore is not
  in Allowed paths). build-coastline.mjs now writes apps/web/public/geo/garis-pantai.json; java-coastline.json is B's.
- regions package.json: "sideEffects": false. Checked: the client chunk that lists provinces (subscription form imports
  the root entry until B moves it to ./meta) has no polygons, 21 kB.
- package-lock.json was written by npm 11 (system npm 9.2 crashes on install); gate `npm ci` (npm 9.2) passed with it.
